ChordForge — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026

The short version: ChordForge does not collect, store, transmit or sell your personal data. Everything you make stays on your own device.

What ChordForge does

ChordForge generates chord progressions and exports them as MIDI files. The music theory engine, the audio playback and the MIDI export all run locally in your browser. Your progressions are never uploaded anywhere.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, names or email addresses.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers or third-party trackers.
  • No browsing history, page content, cookies or data from any website you visit.
  • No progressions, MIDI files or any other creative work you produce.

The one network request

If you purchase ChordForge Pro, the extension sends your licence key to our verification endpoint at money-talks-platform.moneytalks.workers.dev over HTTPS, solely to confirm the key is valid. That request contains the licence key and nothing else — no personal information, no device identifiers, and none of your musical work.

If you never buy Pro, the extension makes no network requests at all and works completely offline.

Payment

Purchases are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen, handled or stored by ChordForge. Stripe's handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policy.

Local storage

ChordForge uses your browser's local storage to remember your settings and, for Pro users, your saved progressions. This data stays on your device. Removing the extension removes it.

Children

ChordForge is a general-audience music tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Limited Use disclosure

ChordForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ChordForge does not collect, transmit or use user data for any purpose other than the single purpose described above, and does not transfer, sell or use such data for advertising, credit assessment or lending.

Changes
